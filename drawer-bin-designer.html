Drawer Bin Designer

Bins that fill your drawer edge to edge, ready to 3D print.

⬇ Offline version

1 Drawer inside size

Measure the inside of the drawer. Width = left to right, depth = front to back.

2 Start with a grid

Then drag the lines (or the handles in 3D) between bins to resize them. The neighbouring bins adjust so there's never any empty space. Ready-made templates are in step 4.

3 Selected bin

Split into

Click a bin in the top view or in 3D to select it. Double-click it to see it on its own. Type a size and press Enter, and the other bins make room.

4 Additional features

Selected bin
What goes in it? (preview only)

Pick an item to see how many fit in the selected bin. It doesn't change the printed part.

Small separators

All bins

Floor thickness and the floor pattern are set per bin, above. Use "Use for all bins" to copy them to every bin.

3MF files
Templates & sharing

Templates keep your drawer size. Share the code with a friend or post it on MakerWorld, and they can paste it here to get your exact design.

5 Printer & download

Plates
File format

Parts are packed onto as few plates as possible for your printer, one file per plate, ready to slice.
